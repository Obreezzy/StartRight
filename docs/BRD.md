# StartRight: Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Problem
People in Zimbabwe who want to start a business struggle to understand
which registrations, licences, taxes, and deadlines apply to them. The
information is scattered across government documents and is hard to read.

## 2. Users
- Aspiring business owners (e.g. tuckshop, salon, small farm, startup)
- Small business advisers and students who help them

## 3. What StartRight will do
- Answer questions about business registration, tax, and compliance
- Produce a step-by-step checklist with fees, offices, and deadlines
- Cite the source document and its date for every answer

## 4. What StartRight will NOT do
- It is not legal advice. It will say so, and recommend confirming with
  the official office.
- It will not invent fees, section numbers, or deadlines. If the sources
  do not cover something, it will say it does not know.

## 5. Success measures
- Answers match the source documents on a benchmark of 40-50 questions
- Every answer includes a source and date
- Hybrid search outperforms vector-only search on the benchmark

## 6. Constraints
- Zero budget: free tiers and local processing only
- Personal data (ID numbers, addresses) must be masked before reaching
  any third-party LLM
